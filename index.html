PSX Dividend Tracker

All upcoming ex-dates in one place. Click any share to see the dividends it has paid before.

To install on iPhone: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

Owner: update the data

1. Open psxterminal.com/dividends, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C. 2. Paste it below. 3. Press “Update from pasted text”, then “Publish for everyone”.

    Upcoming ex-dates and amounts come from PSX Terminal (not affiliated with PSX). Past history builds up as ex-dates pass (FFC and HUBC also keep earlier records from StockAnalysis). Book closure dates are not in this data. Confirm on the PSX Payouts page before trading. "Last day to buy" is the weekday before the ex-date and does not account for public holidays. Calculator tax rates follow a 2026 Section 150 summary; your CDC statement shows the actual deduction. This is not investment advice.
